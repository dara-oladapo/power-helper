What changed, and why

  • It follows Windows now Theme, accent and title bar all come from the OS. Try the controls above — the real app reads exactly these from the registry and repaints live.
  • Real controls, not drawings The old rocker switch was a content-less templated CheckBox: no focus visual, and nothing for a screen reader to announce but “checkbox”, six times in a row.
  • One column, not four The cards used to reflow into up to four columns. No Windows settings surface multi-columns its own rows — the eye has to re-find the control on every one.
  • Colour means something Exactly one thing here is coloured on purpose: the charge meter turns caution below your own warning threshold. Everything else is neutral.
  • Status leads The battery figure is the thing you opened the window to check, so it gets the largest type on the page and sits at the top.
